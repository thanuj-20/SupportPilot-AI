# Customer Service — Knowledge Base

## Overview
This document covers customer service inquiries including product and service information requests, marketing service details, cloud SaaS connectivity issues, and general account assistance.

---

## Issue: Request for Product or Service Information

### Symptoms
- Customer requesting detailed information about available products or services
- Inquiries about features, advantages, pricing, and terms
- Potential clients evaluating the product portfolio

### Resolution Steps
1. Direct the customer to the product catalog at company.com/products.
2. For financial products, provide the product brochure and terms document.
3. Schedule a product demo by emailing sales@company.com or calling Ext. 3001.
4. A customer service representative will follow up within 1 business day.
5. For enterprise inquiries, escalate to the Account Management team.

---

## Issue: Marketing Services Information Request

### Symptoms
- Customer requesting details about marketing offerings
- Inquiries about brand visibility, customer engagement strategies, and pricing

### Resolution Steps
1. Share the marketing services brochure available at company.com/marketing.
2. Provide a summary of available packages: Basic, Professional, and Enterprise.
3. Schedule a consultation call with the marketing team.
4. Email marketing@company.com with the customer's specific requirements.

## Issue: Cloud SaaS Connectivity Issues

### Symptoms
- Sporadic connectivity difficulties with the cloud-native SaaS system
- Suspected Kubernetes orchestration resource distribution issues
- Issue persists after restarting services and reviewing deployment logs

### Resolution Steps
1. Check the SaaS platform status page for active incidents.
2. Review Kubernetes pod logs for OOMKilled or CrashLoopBackOff errors.
3. Scale up the affected deployment: `kubectl scale deployment <name> --replicas=3`
4. Check resource quotas and limits in the namespace configuration.
5. Escalate to the DevOps/Platform Engineering team if the issue persists.
6. Provide deployment logs and error messages when escalating.

## Issue: Account Information and Record Updates

### Symptoms
- Customer requesting updates to account or organisational records
- Recent changes in company structure need to be reflected in the system

### Resolution Steps
1. Submit an account update request via the customer portal.
2. Provide supporting documentation (company registration, authorisation letter).
3. Account updates are processed within 3-5 business days.
4. Contact your dedicated account manager for urgent updates.
