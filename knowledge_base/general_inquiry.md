# General Inquiry — Knowledge Base

## Overview
This document covers general inquiries including organisational structure updates, product and service information requests, and service operations clarification.

---

## Issue: Organisational Structure Update Request

### Symptoms
- Current records reflect outdated organisational information
- Recent changes in company structure need to be updated in the system
- Marketing agency or department has undergone significant changes

### Resolution Steps
1. Submit an Organisation Update Request via the customer portal.
2. Provide the following: company name, registration number, updated structure details, and authorised signatory.
3. Attach supporting documents: updated company registration, board resolution, or official letterhead.
4. Updates are processed within 5 business days.
5. A confirmation email will be sent once the records are updated.
6. Contact your account manager for urgent updates.

## Issue: Product and Service Information Request

### Symptoms
- Customer seeking comprehensive details about products and services
- Requesting brochures, pricing lists, and relevant information
- Evaluating potential acquisitions or partnerships

### Resolution Steps
1. Visit company.com/products for the full product catalogue.
2. Download the product brochure at company.com/resources/brochures.
3. Pricing lists are available at company.com/pricing (login required for enterprise pricing).
4. For a personalised information pack, email info@company.com with your requirements.
5. A sales representative will respond within 1 business day.

---

## Issue: Service Operations Clarification

### Symptoms
- Customer finds service information on the website vague or unclear
- Specific aspects of service operations are difficult to understand
- Need clarification before making a purchasing decision

### Resolution Steps
1. Visit the FAQ page at company.com/faq for answers to common questions.
2. Review the Service Level Agreement (SLA) document at company.com/sla.
3. For specific clarifications, submit a query via the contact form at company.com/contact.
4. A customer service representative will provide a detailed explanation within 2 business days.
5. Live chat is available on the website Mon-Fri 9am-5pm for immediate assistance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I create an account?**
A: Visit company.com/register and complete the registration form. Verification email sent within 5 minutes.

**Q: How do I contact support?**
A: Email support@company.com, call Ext. 1000, or use the live chat on our website.

**Q: Where can I find documentation?**
A: All documentation is available at docs.company.com. Login required for enterprise documentation.

**Q: How do I escalate an unresolved issue?**
A: Reply to your existing ticket and request escalation, or email escalations@company.com with your ticket ID.

**Q: What are your support hours?**
A: Standard support: Mon-Fri 8am-8pm. Critical/P1 support: 24/7.
