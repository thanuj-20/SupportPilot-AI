# Returns and Exchanges — Knowledge Base

## Overview
This document covers the returns and exchanges process, product compatibility inquiries, investment platform queries, and smart home product integration questions.

---

## Issue: How to Return a Purchase

### Symptoms
- Customer wants to return a recently purchased item
- Unsure about the return procedure and required steps

### Resolution Steps
1. Log in to your account at company.com/account.
2. Navigate to Orders > Order History and select the order to return.
3. Click "Request Return" and select the reason for return.
4. Print the prepaid return shipping label provided.
5. Pack the item securely in its original packaging if possible.
6. Drop off the package at any authorised courier location within 14 days.
7. Refund will be processed within 5-7 business days after the item is received.

---

## Return Policy Summary
- Returns accepted within 30 days of purchase
- Item must be in original condition and packaging
- Digital products and downloadable software are non-returnable
- Customised or personalised items are non-returnable
- Proof of purchase required for all returns

## Issue: Product Compatibility Inquiry

### Symptoms
- Customer wants to know if a product is compatible with their existing setup
- Smart home integration compatibility questions (Alexa, Google Assistant, Apple HomeKit)

### Resolution Steps
1. Visit the product compatibility page at company.com/compatibility.
2. Check the supported smart home ecosystems listed for each product.
3. For Amazon Alexa: enable the company skill in the Alexa app.
4. For Google Assistant: link the account via Google Home app > Add Device.
5. For Apple HomeKit: scan the HomeKit setup code on the product packaging.
6. Contact Product Support if compatibility issues persist after setup.

## Issue: Exchange Request

### Symptoms
- Customer received a defective or incorrect item
- Customer wants to exchange for a different size, colour, or model

### Resolution Steps
1. Contact Customer Service within 14 days of receiving the item.
2. Provide the order number, item description, and reason for exchange.
3. A prepaid return label will be emailed within 24 hours.
4. The replacement item will be shipped once the original is received.
5. Express exchange available for Critical defects — contact Ext. 3002.
