## Overview

## Issue: VPN-Router Connectivity Affecting Product Integration

### Symptoms
- VPN-router connectivity disruption impacting remote devices
- EMR integrations and telemedicine systems affected
- Restarting devices and resetting router has not resolved the issue

### Resolution Steps
1. Verify the VPN client version is up to date.
2. Check router firmware and update if necessary.
3. Confirm VPN gateway settings match the IT-provided configuration.
4. Test connectivity with a direct wired connection to isolate Wi-Fi issues.
5. Escalate to IT Support if EMR or critical integrations remain affected.

## Issue: Unclear Product Features or Capabilities

### Symptoms
- Customer unsure how to use a specific product feature
- Feature behaves differently than expected
- No clear documentation available

### Resolution Steps
1. Visit the product documentation portal at docs.company.com.
2. Search for the specific feature name in the knowledge base.
3. Watch the product tutorial videos available on the support page.
4. Contact Product Support with your product name, version, and a description of the issue.
5. A product specialist will respond within 4 business hours.

## Issue: Product Not Working as Expected

### Symptoms
- Product behaves unexpectedly or inconsistently
- User unable to clearly describe the issue
- Intermittent failures with no clear pattern

### Resolution Steps
1. Document the exact steps that lead to the issue.
2. Note the time, frequency, and any error messages displayed.
3. Check if the issue occurs on multiple devices or just one.
4. Submit a support ticket with screenshots or screen recordings if possible.
5. Product Support will triage and respond within 4 business hours.
