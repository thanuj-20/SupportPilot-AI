# Billing and Payments — Knowledge Base

## Overview
This document covers billing inquiries, payment inconsistencies, billing cycle clarification, payment options, and subscription management for enterprise accounts.

---

## Issue: Billing Inconsistencies or Incorrect Charges

### Symptoms
- Charges on the account do not match the expected amount
- Unexpected fees or overages appear on the invoice
- Billing cycle dates are unclear

### Resolution Steps
1. Log in to the billing portal at billing.company.com.
2. Navigate to Invoices > History and download the latest invoice.
3. Compare the invoice line items against your service agreement.
4. Check for any add-on services, overages, or pro-rata charges.
5. If a discrepancy is found, submit a billing dispute under Billing > Disputes.
6. The billing team will review and respond within 2 business days.

---

## Issue: Understanding the Billing Cycle

### Symptoms
- Customer unsure about billing cycle start and end dates
- Confusion about when charges are applied

### Resolution Steps
1. Log in to the billing portal and go to Settings > Billing Cycle.
2. Your billing cycle start date is shown on your account dashboard.
3. Invoices are generated on the 1st of each month for the previous month's usage.
4. Payment is due within 30 days of invoice generation.
5. Contact billing@company.com for a detailed billing cycle explanation.

---

## Issue: Payment Not Confirmed or Delayed

### Symptoms
- Payment submitted but not reflected in the account
- Inconsistencies in billed amounts
- Delays in payment confirmation emails

### Resolution Steps
1. Check your bank or card statement to confirm the payment was processed.
2. Allow up to 3 business days for bank transfers to reflect.
3. Log in to the billing portal and check Payment History for the transaction status.
4. If the payment shows as pending for more than 3 days, contact billing support.
5. Provide the transaction reference number when contacting support.

---

## Issue: QuickBooks Online Subscription Billing

### Symptoms
- Unclear about QuickBooks Online subscription plans and pricing
- Need to understand available tiers and enterprise options

### Resolution Steps
1. Visit quickbooks.intuit.com/pricing for current plan details.
2. For enterprise pricing, contact your account manager directly.
3. Billing for QuickBooks Online is monthly or annual — annual plans offer a discount.
4. To upgrade or downgrade, go to Settings > Subscription in QuickBooks Online.

## Accepted Payment Methods
- Credit/Debit Card: Visa, Mastercard, American Express
- Bank Transfer: ACH (US), SWIFT (International)
- Purchase Order: Available for Enterprise accounts only
- PayPal: Available for accounts under $10,000/month

## Refund Policy
- Refunds available within 30 days of charge for unused services
- Partial refunds calculated on a pro-rata basis
- One-time setup fees are non-refundable
- Submit refund requests at billing.company.com/refunds
