## Issue: Product Compatibility for Marketing Agencies

### Symptoms
- Marketing agency evaluating product adoption to streamline processes
- Need to confirm compatibility with existing marketing workflows and tools

### Resolution Steps
1. Contact the Pre-Sales team at presales@company.com for a compatibility assessment.
2. Provide details of your current marketing stack (CRM, automation tools, analytics platforms).
3. A pre-sales engineer will conduct a technical fit analysis within 2 business days.
4. Request a proof-of-concept (POC) trial for 30 days at no cost.
5. Integration guides for popular marketing platforms are at developer.company.com/integrations.
