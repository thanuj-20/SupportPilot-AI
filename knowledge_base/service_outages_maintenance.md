# Service Outages and Maintenance — Knowledge Base

## Overview
This document covers service disruption reporting, scheduled maintenance notifications, EMR/PACS network failures, security compromise procedures, and big data system maintenance.

---

## Issue: Multiple System Service Disruptions

### Symptoms
- Multiple systems disrupted simultaneously affecting project operations
- Client interactions and internal workflows impacted
- Deadlines at risk due to system unavailability

### Resolution Steps
1. Immediately report the disruption to IT Support — this is a High/Critical priority.
2. Identify which systems are affected and document the scope.
3. Check the internal status dashboard at status.company.com.
4. IT will initiate the Incident Response Plan within 15 minutes of a Critical report.
5. Updates will be communicated every 30 minutes via email and the status page.
6. Business continuity procedures: switch to offline/backup systems where available.

---

## Issue: Scheduled Maintenance Notification Request

### Symptoms
- Customer requesting advance notice of upcoming maintenance windows
- Need to plan around downtime for big data repositories and analytical models

### Resolution Steps
1. Subscribe to maintenance notifications at status.company.com/subscribe.
2. Maintenance windows are scheduled on the 2nd Sunday of each month, 2am-6am.
3. Emergency maintenance notifications are sent at least 2 hours in advance.
4. Contact your account manager to receive personalised maintenance schedules.
5. Critical systems have a minimum 48-hour advance notice policy.

---

## Issue: EMR/PACS Network Failure and Security Compromise

### Symptoms
- Severe disruption affecting EMR/PACS connections
- Network failure suspected with possible security compromise
- SOC DevSecOps teams have identified immediate vulnerabilities

### Resolution Steps
1. IMMEDIATELY isolate affected systems from the network.
2. Alert the Security Operations Centre (SOC): security@company.com | Ext. 9001.
3. Do NOT attempt to restore systems without SOC authorisation.
4. Preserve all logs for forensic analysis — do not clear or overwrite.
5. Activate the Incident Response Plan and notify the CISO within 30 minutes.
6. Regulatory notification may be required within 72 hours (HIPAA/GDPR).
7. Post-incident review will be conducted within 5 business days.

---

## Issue: Outage Affecting Account Access

### Symptoms
- System outage blocking access to account settings
- Users unable to log in or manage their accounts

### Resolution Steps
1. Check status.company.com for the current outage status and ETR.
2. IT Support will provide workaround access for critical operations.
3. Subscribe to SMS/email alerts for real-time outage updates.
4. Contact IT Support if you need emergency access during an outage.

---

## Incident Severity Levels
- **P1 Critical**: Full service outage, security breach — 15-min response, 4-hour resolution target
- **P2 High**: Major feature unavailable — 1-hour response, 8-hour resolution target
- **P3 Medium**: Partial degradation — 4-hour response, 24-hour resolution target
- **P4 Low**: Minor issue, workaround available — 8-hour response, 72-hour resolution target
